# Figma lock

Only one Claude session or development plugin may write to the Figma file at a time. Read [docs/collaboration.md](docs/collaboration.md) §4.

| Status | Held by | What | Since |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | — | — | — |

To take the lock: pull, replace the row with your name, what you're running and the time, commit and push. To release: set it back to **Free**, commit and push.
